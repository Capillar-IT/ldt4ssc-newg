We held the **NeWG+ Madrid Operational Kick-Off** bringing together **Mercamadrid**, wholesale and retail sector associations (**AEMPM**, **ADEPESCA**, **COMAPES**), and the five participating municipal areas of **Ayuntamiento de Madrid** (*Oficina Digital / DGOD, Comercio, Movilidad, Madrid Salud, and Medio Ambiente y Energía*) to align on the 18-month rollout across Mercamadrid and the municipal market network.

## Phased territorial rollout: From Mercamadrid to 5 pilot markets

During the session, the consortium and municipal stakeholders validated the four-phase corridor activation plan connecting **Mercamadrid** with **5 pilot municipal markets** (*Maravillas, Vallehermoso, Tetuán, Chamartín, and Ventas*) as territorial anchors for scaling across Madrid's 46 municipal markets:

- **Phase F1 (M1–M4 · Gate H1)**: Activation of the initial **Mercamadrid → Mercado de Maravillas** B2B corridor with a first cohort of 6 wholesalers and minimum EUI/corridor service-anchor instrumentation.
- **Phase F2 (M5–M7 · Gate H2)**: Full B2B service operation at **Mercado de Maravillas** (12 wholesalers), calibrating end-to-end batch traceability, Digital Product Passport (DPP) views, and data-space governance policies.
- **Phase F3 (M8–M10 · Gate H3)**: Extension to the multi-market corridor covering **Maravillas, Vallehermoso, and Tetuán** (18 wholesalers) with B2B2C capillarity and H3 Smart Mesh calibration across markets with and without solar PV / EV charging infrastructure.
- **Phase F4 (M11–M18 · Gate H4)**: Urban ramification to **Chamartín** and activation of the second corridor anchor at **Mercado de Ventas** (reaching 34 wholesalers), completing integration with Madrid's **Twin-of-Twins (*Gemelo de Gemelos*)**, **City OS**, and **Smart Urban Spaces (EUIs)**.

## Alignment across the 7-step operational flow and municipal areas

We reviewed the end-to-end 7-step operational flow—from multichannel order capture (**CoCo AI** on WhatsApp) and assisted warehouse preparation (**Stocker WMS** + edge IoT/RFID) to shared multi-species refrigerated transport, **H3 Smart Mesh** context enrichment, governed publication via **MIRA / InterFlux**, and LDT simulation—confirming the value return for each municipal domain:

- **Commerce (*Dirección General de Comercio*)**: Restoring logistics convenience and data sovereignty to local wholesalers and neighborhood retailers without forcing digital complexity or platform lock-in.
- **Mobility (*Área de Movilidad y Transportes*)**: Consolidating multi-supplier loads (proven 5:1 vehicle reduction ratio in the 2020–2021 baseline), integrating with **DUM 360** loading bay reservations, and simulating street restrictions before implementation.
- **Public Health (*Madrid Salud*)**: Validating multi-species refrigerated compartmentalisation (fish, meat, produce) and providing instant, role-authorized access to auditable Digital Product Passport (DPP) cold-chain and hygiene evidence.
- **Environment & Energy (*Medio Ambiente y Energía*)**: Measuring before/after impact (kWh per delivery, solar self-consumption, grid draw, and avoided emissions) across markets equipped with photovoltaic panels and EV chargers versus non-equipped nodes.
- **Digital Office (*DGOD*)**: Operationalizing **MIRA (*Minimum Interoperability Reference Architecture*)** to bridge live market operations with the Mercamadrid/Mobilities4EU data space, EDINT, and the municipal Twin-of-Twins.

## Immediate 30/60/90-day actions

The operational working groups are now executing the 30/60/90-day onboarding sequence with **AEMPM**, **ADEPESCA**, and **COMAPES** to capture the baseline KPIs and complete the first **Mercamadrid–Maravillas** live workflow demonstrations ahead of **Gate H1**.
