## Part I — EMT Madrid Open Data & MobilityLabs Analysis

### 1.1 Strategic Value of EMT Datasets for the NeWG+ H3 Smart Mesh

While `informo.madrid.es` provides road loop-sensor telemetry and `datos.madrid.es` provides DUM 360 loading bays, **EMT Madrid operates four critical urban subsystems** that directly enrich the **NeWG+ H3 Smart Mesh** (Resolutions 8, 9, and 10):

1. **Municipal Tow Truck (`Retirada de vehículos por la grúa municipal` — Dataset `78f8a706` / `300118-0`) as a Curbside Friction Sensor:**
   - EMT manages Madrid's Municipal Tow Truck service and publishes both **live XML feeds** (`tablavehiculos.xml`, `tablaturnos.xml`) and **historical monthly CSV logs** (`2025entradas.csv`, `2026entradas.csv`, `2025salidas.csv`) detailing towed vehicles, timestamps, streets, and infraction categories.
   - **H3 Resolution 10 Application:** High tow-truck intervention density around specific **Municipal Markets** or commercial streets identifies structural **illegal double-parking and loading-bay invasion**, allowing the H3 Smart Mesh to apply a realistic **curb-access friction penalty** even when DUM 360 ticket logs appear under-saturated.
2. **EMT Off-Street & Deterrent Car Parks (`Aparcamientos EMT` — Dataset `0f3310f5`, Hourly CSV `e8798dd9` & OpenAPI Block 5):**
   - EMT publishes the geospatial inventory (`CSV`, `GeoJSON`, `KML`, `SHP`) of all EMT-managed car parks, **15 monthly CSVs of hourly total vs. available spaces** (`parking_places_hourly_202608.csv`), and the **real-time free-space REST endpoint** (`GET /{version}/citymad/places/parkings/availability/`, where `v3`/`v4` add deterrent parking *parkings disuasorios* and total capacity metrics).
   - **H3 Resolution 10 Application:** Enables **micro-hub buffer routing** — when surface DUM loading bays at a Municipal Market reach 100% saturation, light commercial vans can be dynamically routed to adjacent underground EMT car parks with verified real-time slot availability.
3. **EMT Surface Bus Corridor Telemetry (`GTFS`, `GTFS-RT Protobuf` & OpenAPI Block 3 `BUSEMTMAD`):**
   - EMT buses traverse Madrid's primary commercial arteries on dedicated bus/taxi lanes. By combining static **GTFS** (`gtfs_emt.zip`), **GTFS-RT Service Alerts Protobuf** (`/v1/bus/servicealerts/proto`), and **real-time arrival estimations** (`/v2/` and `/v3/transport/busemtmad/stops/{stopId}/arrives/`), NeWG+ gains a secondary surface-speed and disruption sensor on avenues lacking induction loops.
4. **BiciMAD GBFS v2 & BiciPARK for Last-Mile Sustainable Micro-Logistics (`70341916` & `ebae8103`):**
   - EMT provides an **unauthenticated, open-standard GBFS v2.2 real-time feed** (`https://madrid.publicbikesystem.net/customer/gbfs/v2/gbfs.json`, updated every **2 seconds**) alongside **BiciPARK** secure bicycle lockers (`biciparkstations.csv`).
   - **H3 Resolution 10 Application:** Supports last-mile **cargo-bike and Light Electric Vehicle (LEV)** dispatch modeling from the 5 pilot Municipal Markets (*Maravillas, Vallehermoso, Tetuán, Chamartín, Ventas*) to nearby HORECA and home-delivery destinations.
5. **Direct Sovereign Data Space Alignment (`EMT Madrid Data Space` ⇄ `MIRA / InterFlux / SEAMWARE`):**
   - EMT's new **Data Space** (`https://mobilitylabs.emtmadrid.es/data-space`, NextGenerationEU / Plan de Recuperación) explicitly enforces **ODRL policies**, **EU Data Act** compliance, and **automated Smart Contracts**. This matches the exact **ODRL + ABAC / FIWARE / EDC** governance stack deployed by **capillar IT** and **SEAMWARE** in **NeWG+**, enabling federated connector-to-connector data exchange under **OASC MIM3 (Exchanging Data)** and **MIM6 (Securing Data)**.

## Part IV — Additional Datasets for the H3 Mesh ETA & Service-Time Algorithm

To achieve high-precision **End-to-End Delivery ETA**, the H3 Smart Mesh models total arrival time at destination $i$ across **four additive components** plus **Cold-Chain Thermal Drift**:

$$\text{ETA}_i = t_0 + \underbrace{\sum_{e \in \text{Route}} \tau_{\text{drive}}(e, t)}_{\text{1. Link Traversal}} + \underbrace{\sum_{v \in \text{Route}} \tau_{\text{signal}}(v, t)}_{\text{2. Intersection Delay}} + \underbrace{\tau_{\text{curb}}(h_{10}, t)}_{\text{3. Curb Search & Queue}} + \underbrace{\tau_{\text{handover}}(i, t)}_{\text{4. Last-50m Handover}}$$

Auditing all **675 datasets** across `datos.madrid.es` and `datos.emtmadrid.es` reveals **18 additional non-obvious datasets** (spanning *Meteorology, Public Works, Fines, Sidewalk GIS, Street Markets, and Sports/Cultural Agendas*) that significantly reduce ETA prediction error:
