# NeWG+ Documentation

This folder contains the general project documentation for the **NeWG+** pilot (*New Wholesale Market Generation: cities digital infrastructure implementation through sustainable logistics and commerce*, LDT4SSC Work Strand 1).

## Contents

- **[`architecture.md`](architecture.md)** — Differentiated two-city functional & technical architecture (*Market-to-City* in Madrid and *Territory-to-City* in Codogno), MIRA / InterFlux data-space governance, H3 Smart Mesh, Digital Product Passport (DPP) continuity, and OASC MIMs Plus compliance (`MIM0`–`MIM8`).
- **[`madrid-open-data-h3-smart-mesh.md`](madrid-open-data-h3-smart-mesh.md)** — Technical study and mapping of Madrid's Open Data (`datos.madrid.es`, `informo.madrid.es`, `mc30.es`) live and structural datasets powering the multi-resolution H3 Spatial Smart Mesh (Resolutions 8, 9, and 10).
- **[`madrid-emt-and-open-data-catalog.md`](madrid-emt-and-open-data-catalog.md)** — Master analysis and exhaustive catalog of **EMT Madrid Open Data (`datos.emtmadrid.es`, 22 datasets)**, **EMT MobilityLabs OpenAPI (`openapi.emtmadrid.es`, 52 REST endpoints + 8 GBFS v2 feeds)**, **EMT Madrid Data Space (`ODRL`)**, and **all 109 Transport/Mobility + 22 Cross-Domain datasets in `datos.madrid.es`**.

## Pilot Implementation Phases & Validation Gates (18 Months: Aug 2026 – Feb 2028)

The NeWG+ pilot follows a progressive 4-phase rollout aligned with the LDT4SSC methodology (*Explore → Validate → Define → Implement*):

| Phase / Gate | Timeline | Madrid (*Market-to-City* Flagship) | Codogno (*Territory-to-City* Corridor Twin) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1 · Gate H1** | **M1–M4** | **Initial reliable service validation (`Mercamadrid → Mercado de Maravillas`)**: Baseline cohort activation, minimum EUI/corridor service-anchor instrumentation at Mercado de Maravillas, initial governed publication & KPI baseline. | **Baseline & Observatory Setup**: Municipal mobility/access baseline, actor mapping (≥ 20 producers/operators), and local agrifood-flow observatory setup. |
| **F2 · Gate H2** | **M5–M7** | **Complete Mercado de Maravillas B2B service**: Full B2B service operation and consolidation at Mercado de Maravillas, DPP continuity across receiving/preparation/dispatch/POD, and Shelter Gateway policies. | **Corridor Modelling & Local Smart Mesh**: POLIMI-led corridor context modelling, travel-time/access simulations, and decision gate for optional light NeWG probe. |
| **F3 · Gate H3** | **M8–M10** | **Mercado de Maravillas B2B2C capillarity & H3 Smart Mesh calibration**: Last-mile B2B2C/HORECA distribution around Mercado de Maravillas, H3 Smart Mesh calibration (DUM 360 curb friction), and selective external data ingestion (Green IT). | **Scenario Execution & Shared Dispatch Test**: Execution of ≥ 6 before/after urban access and dispatch scenarios, plus pilot-limited shared-dispatch probe where validated. |
| **F4 · Gate H4** | **M11–M18** | **Twin-of-Twins Integration & City-Wide Replication Blueprint**: Full integration of the Mercamadrid–Maravillas corridor with EUI/City OS/Gemelo de Gemelos and delivery of the open replication package for Madrid's municipal market network. | **Transfer Package & Continuity**: Consolidation of the 4 bounded shared services, validation report, Milano/Sogemi compatibility path, and WS1 transferability package. |
